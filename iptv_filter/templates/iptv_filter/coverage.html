A ticked channel gets guide data when its tvg-id is the same as a channel id in the EPG file. Players compare exactly, so when your provider only differs in letter case (npo1.nl vs NPO1.nl) the playlist this app serves carries the EPG's spelling{% if not coverage.fix_enabled %} (this correction is switched off by IPTV_FIX_EPG_IDS){% endif %}. This page shows which ticked channels still get no guide, and why.

You can decide by hand for any channel below: type or pick the guide channel it should use and press Use, or press No guide for channels you know have none (they then stop counting against the score). The playlist served by this app uses your choice right away. When a channel name closely resembles an EPG channel, that channel is pre-filled as a suggestion; check it before pressing Use.
